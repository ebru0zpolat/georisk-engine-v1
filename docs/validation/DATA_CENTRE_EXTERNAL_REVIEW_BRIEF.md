# Independent Data Centre Engineering Review Brief

**Status:** reviewer unassigned; no assurance or sign-off is claimed.

## Reviewer profile

The reviewer should have demonstrable data-centre mechanical/electrical
engineering or climate-resilience experience and must disclose financial,
employment, investment and project-delivery conflicts. The reviewer must not be
the author of the methodology under review.

## Review scope

The reviewer is asked to challenge:

- cooling-type logic and dry-/wet-bulb design-limit comparisons;
- treatment of water dependency and provider-classified water stress;
- flood-depth versus finished-floor logic and missing local protection details;
- backup-duration versus site/grid outage evidence;
- pathway aggregation into downtime and business-interruption indicators;
- evidence licensing, cutoffs and provenance;
- conditions required before any monetary output can be released;
- that any backtest names `app.validation.gate1.run_comparison` only;
- wording boundaries for engineering and EU regulatory claims.

## Required artefacts

- signed conflict and independence declaration;
- review report with findings classified as blocking/major/minor;
- exact methodology version and code commit reviewed;
- SHA-256 digest of the final review report;
- approval or rejection decision and validity period;
- response-to-findings log with unresolved items visible.

## Non-claims

Reviewer engagement is not equivalent to certification. GeoRisk must not publish
the reviewer's name, employer or approval status without permission and evidence.
No approved review exists until the signed artefacts are in the evidence registry.
