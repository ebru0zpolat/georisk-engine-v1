# Regulation (EU) 2024/1364 — GeoRisk Product Boundary

**Legal status:** product-scope control, not legal advice.

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1364 establishes the first phase of a
common Union data-centre rating scheme and defines sustainability information and
KPIs to be reported by in-scope operators.

## What GeoRisk v0.1 can support

- traceable heat, flood and water-stress site evidence;
- data-centre vulnerability field completeness;
- scenario comparison and evidence-gap identification;
- lender, insurer and investor physical-risk due diligence.

## What remains outside GeoRisk v0.1

- measured energy consumption and power-utilisation data;
- server-room and facility temperature set points;
- waste-heat reuse measurements;
- measured water consumption and water-usage effectiveness;
- operator reporting submissions, filing responsibility and legal eligibility;
- verification or assurance of reported operational KPIs.

## Mandatory claim control

GeoRisk outputs may be described as supporting physical-climate evidence. They
must not be described as proving or certifying compliance with Regulation (EU)
2024/1364. The machine-readable boundary is available at:

`GET /v1/risk/data-centre/contract`
