# GEO-76 — TSRS 2 climate-risk review: claim guardrails

**Status:** Guardrails for a review draft in progress (Linear GEO-76). Not the review itself.
**Scope:** Erdemir / İsdemir (Ereğli + İskenderun) steel-sector review against TSRS 2, with
IFRS S2 / TCFD and the GHG Protocol as the traceability anchors.
**Repo evidence it constrains:** `data/evidence/erdemir_2024_evidence.{json,md}` (case
`erdemir_2024`, generated by `scripts/generate_erdemir_evidence.py` from
`app/services/validation_service.py`).

---

## Why this file exists

GEO-76 asks for three quantification modules, a four-tier evidence hierarchy, steel-sector
benchmarking, standards traceability and independent review. The review draft was being
written in a session without repository access. Three claim limits identified there have to
be enforced in the repo too, and one of them pointed at a Linear issue whose meaning has
changed. This file records the limits, their corrected pointers, and where the repo already
does or does not comply.

The wording rule in `docs/methodology/GEORISK_ENGINE_METHODOLOGY.md` (regulatory-coverage
note and its "Avoid" list) applies
throughout: outputs *support selected* TSRS 2 / IFRS S2 evidence requirements. Never
"meets / satisfies / complies with".

## Guardrail 1 — Erdemir and İsdemir are one consolidated observation, not two peers

- **Limit.** Erdemir's TSRS 2025 report consolidates İsdemir. Any figure taken from that
  report (production, Scope 1+2 intensity, water stress, CBAM band) is one observation of one
  reporting entity. It must not be counted twice, used as a two-point "peer" benchmark, or
  presented as an independent cross-check of itself.
- **Repo state: compliant.** The evidence trail already treats Ereğli + İskenderun as a single
  weighted signal (`ERD-2024-PROD-001`, `ERD-2024-WATER-001`). Site-level water stress
  (`ERD-2024-WATER-002/003`) is a *decomposition* of the consolidated figure and is flagged
  amber as a data gap because the company discloses no site split. Keep that framing.
- **What a peer benchmark must be.** A different reporting entity on the same basis (World
  Steel BOF intensity is the only such benchmark in the trail today) or a second Turkish BOF
  producer's own TSRS filing. Both need their own provenance record before use.

## Guardrail 2 — 2024 and 2025 emissions are not on the same measurement basis

- **Limit.** The 2024 figure in the Erdemir 2025 report is a comparison column produced on a
  different measurement basis from the 2025 figure. (Basis change identified in the GEO-76
  review work against the TSRS 2025 filing; the repo evidence file had recorded a basis change
  only for water stress. Cite the filing's restatement note in the draft.) A 2024→2025 change in intensity or
  absolute emissions cannot be described as improvement or deterioration until the company
  publishes a restated 2024 figure on the 2025 basis.
- **Repo state: corrected in this change.** `ERD-2024-INT-001` previously justified its amber
  flag with "trend worsening 2024→2025". That was the overclaim this guardrail prohibits.
  Both intensity records now flag amber as a **comparability gap** (single-year benchmark
  read is within ±15%; the year-over-year direction is unverifiable). Flag colour and summary
  counts are unchanged (5 green, 5 amber, 0 red), so the API and tests are stable; only the
  rationale text changed.
- **Rule for future records.** A directional trend across a basis change is a data gap, not a
  finding. Encode it as `directionally_consistent=False` with a comparability note, never as
  "worsening".

## Guardrail 3 — flood / heat outputs are not verified loss or downtime estimates

- **Limit.** Physical-hazard outputs (flood water levels, heat-stress indices, EAL magnitudes)
  are proxy or scenario outputs. They cannot be described as verified loss, verified
  downtime, or validated expected-loss estimates until a preregistered real-event backtest
  against licensed observed-loss data closes with an independent reviewer.
- **Corrected pointer.** The draft conditioned this on "GEO-105's observed-loss backtest
  closing". That is stale. Per `GEORISK_PRODUCTION_ROADMAP_2026.md` (reconciliation of
  3 September 2026, table row GEO-105, and Stage 5): **GEO-105 was redefined and closed on
  2026-08-31 as the self-attested MVP validation tier.** It no longer carries the real-event
  backtest. That gate is now the roadmap **Stage 5 real-event scientific validation gate**,
  which is unowned and has **no Linear issue**. The correct wording is:

  > …until the Stage 5 real-event validation gate closes (a named event, licensed
  > observed-loss data, a preregistered sample and exclusions, a fixed comparison cutoff and
  > an independent reviewer). GEO-105 being Done does not satisfy this.

- **Action outstanding.** Open the Stage 5 gate as its own Linear issue before any backtest
  work or any "validated" wording is used. This cannot be done from the repository.
- **Repo state.** `model_validated_for_use_case` remains `false` (roadmap §1). The three
  other docs that named GEO-105 as the backtest gate were repointed in this change:
  `docs/data-source-operational-gate.md`, `docs/nl_pilot/forward-horizon-scenarios.md`,
  `docs/nl_pilot/real-hazard-eal-pipeline.md`.

## Evidence-tier language to reuse in the draft

| Tier | What the repo can currently say | What it cannot say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reported figure | "Erdemir TSRS 2025 reports X (assurance: limited / none)" | that GeoRisk reproduced X |
| Independent signal | "GeoRisk proxy gives Y from source Z (confidence c)" | that Y is measured |
| Divergence flag | "Y is within / outside ±15% of X; flag" | that the flag is a compliance finding |
| Validated | nothing yet — Stage 5 gate open | "validated", "verified loss", "verified downtime" |
