Version v1.0 · June 2026 · Internal / partner-facing draft
Registry: app/core/data_source_registry.py · API: GET /v1/data-sources

Disclaimer. Satellite and climate-derived proxies support decision-grade risk intelligence. They are not regulatory proof, certified emissions reporting, or ground-truth production measurements. Mask policies and legal framing: docs/turkiye/cco-audit-methodology.md.

1. Letter of Intent — Data Partnership Posture

GeoRisk Engine intends to operate exclusively through official programmatic access to public and licensed Earth observation products:

  • Copernicus CDS — ORAS5 analysis-ready Zarr (ocean / climate context)
  • Google Earth Engine — VIIRS, Sentinel-5P OFFL L3, Landsat Collection 2 Level-2
  • NOAA / NASA / USGS / ESA — product terms respected via GEE and CDS gateways

Credentials (ORAS5_ZARR_URL, GEE_PROJECT) remain environment-scoped. No secrets or project IDs are committed to the repository.

3. Production Data Sources

ORAS5 Ocean Reanalysis intent

Provider: Copernicus CDS / ECMWF ORAS5

Connector: app/connectors/cds_oras5.py

Features: SST trend (°C/decade), SST anomaly z-score, sea-level trend (mm/year), ocean variability index

Fallback: Deterministic stub when ORAS5_ZARR_URL is unset (CI-safe).

VIIRS Night-Time Lights (GEE) active

Dataset: NASA/VIIRS/002/VNP46A2

Band: Gap_Filled_DNB_BRDF_Corrected_NTL

Connector: app/connectors/gee.py

Use: Industrial activity proxy vs configurable 30-year climatological baseline.

Sentinel-5P OFFL L3 NO₂ (GEE) active

Dataset: COPERNICUS/S5P/OFFL/L3_NO2

Band: tropospheric_NO2_column_number_density (mol/m²)

Standard: docs/standards/cement-data-acceptance-and-processing.md
